Quick guide
How GongBi Translator works
Your Office file stays in the browser. Extract the Chinese text, let your AI translate it, then rebuild the original file with the English text inserted back into place.
Step 1 Upload Choose a DOCX or PPTX file.
Step 2 Extract Click Extract text. The AI prompt is copied.
Step 3 Translate Paste the prompt into your AI and run it.
Step 4 Paste back Paste the AI response into Step 2.
Step 5 Build & download Rebuild the translated Office file.
What gets extracted

Chinese text is collected from supported Word and PowerPoint text parts. PPTX can also include speaker notes, charts, and SmartArt.

Translation matching

Every text unit has a numeric ID. For Word, highlighted phrases also carry protected inline IDs to preserve their positions after translation.

Formatting preserved

Word highlights are carried through protected AI tags and validated before reconstruction; other Office formatting is retained wherever possible.

Tip: If the AI response stops early, paste the partial result first. GongBi can identify the missing IDs and generate a continuation prompt for only the untranslated text.
